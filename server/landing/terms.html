7. Pickup, delivery and proof of delivery

Drivers record the pickup and delivery in the Driver app. At delivery we collect a photo and the receiver’s signature, and the app records the driver’s location and the time at each stop. You can see the driver’s live location and this proof of delivery in your account and on the order’s tracking link. Anyone you share a tracking link with can see the order’s status, the driver’s first name, photo, vehicle and live location while it’s underway. A delivery is complete when the shipment is handed to a person at the delivery address, or left in a place you or the receiver have directed.
