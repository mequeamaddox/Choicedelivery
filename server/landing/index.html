    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can't find your answer? Call (803) 949-7034 or send us a message.

    How much does a delivery cost?+

    Enter your pickup and delivery addresses in the instant quote to see your price. It's based on the vehicle, the driving distance and the number of stops, and you can open the price details to see exactly what each part is for. You see the full price before you book.

    How fast is Rush delivery?+

    With Rush, your order goes to the front of the line and is driven straight to the drop-off, with no other stops in between. That's about 2 hours for trips within 50 miles of pickup; longer trips take as long as the drive. The rush fee starts at $25 for local trips and is a little more for longer ones. Your quote shows the exact amount.

    Where do you pick up and deliver?+

    We're based in Columbia, SC. We pick up across Columbia and the Midlands and deliver throughout South Carolina, plus nearby North Carolina and Georgia. Pickups farther from Columbia can still be quoted; we'll confirm the job before it's booked.

    What are your hours?+

    8 AM to 10 PM, every day. Call (803) 949-7034 during those hours for anything urgent.

    Do I need an account?+

    Not to get a quote. To book, create a free account at app.choicedeliverysc.com. It takes about a minute, and you can save quotes, book again quickly and see all your deliveries in one place. Businesses can add coworkers to the same account.

    How do I pay?+

    By card when you book, through our secure checkout. If you booked with us by phone, we'll email you a secure payment link. Approved business accounts can be invoiced instead.

    Are there any extra charges?+

    Only if they happen: waiting more than 15 minutes at a stop, a return trip, or a delivery that couldn't be completed (for example, no one was there). We'll email you the reason and amount for any extra charge.

    How do I track my delivery?+

    Use the tracking link we send you, or enter your order number and delivery ZIP under Track a delivery. You can follow the driver on a live map, and every delivery is confirmed with a photo, signature and GPS location.

    What can't you carry?+

    No single piece can weigh more than 75 lbs, and the order has to fit the vehicle you choose. We don't carry hazardous materials, weapons, cash or valuables, live animals, medical waste, or anything illegal. See our Terms for the full list.

    Can I cancel an order?+

    Yes. You can cancel in the app at no charge until a driver accepts the job, and card payments are refunded in full (refunds usually show up within 5 to 10 business days). After a driver has accepted, call us at (803) 949-7034; a charge may apply for work already done.

    What if something is lost or damaged?+

    Tell us within 24 hours of delivery. Our coverage is up to $100 per order unless we agree otherwise before pickup, so if your shipment is worth more, contact us before booking.

    Do I need a shipping label?+

    No, but it helps. Our free shipping form prints a label with a barcode on regular letter-size paper, so no special label printer is needed.

    How can I drive with Choice Delivery?+

    We're always looking for reliable drivers in the Columbia area. Apply here; you'll need to be at least 21 with a valid driver's license and insurance.
